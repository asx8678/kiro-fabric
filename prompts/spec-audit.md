# One-shot specification audit

Perform one bounded pass without delegation. Enumerate every requirement, then mechanically verify its applicable symbols, registrations, configuration, behavior, and validation, attaching repository evidence and the observed validation outcome to each. A requirement is satisfied only when every applicable check has verified evidence and requested validation ran successfully. Otherwise mark its evidence `unverified` or `failed` and report result status `partial` or `failed`; use `success` only for complete coverage. Do not retry, recurse, use model agreement as proof, or claim certification.
